Question: What you think when ID changes ?
According to my observation, the ID changed from 1 to 4 because of a temporary loss of track. When the person in the red hoodie walked out of the camera frame (or got blocked), the tracker lost his motion history.

ByteTrack mainly relies on continuous motion to predict where a person will be in the next frame. Because he disappeared for a few seconds, the model forgot his previous identity. When he re-entered the frame, the model couldn't match his face/clothes with the old ID, so it considered him as a brand new person and assigned him a new ID (4).

This is known as an ID Switch and it proves why manual counting (3 people) is different from the tracker's count (4 unique IDs)
